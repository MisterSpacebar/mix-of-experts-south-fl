## Scientific constraints

The same pair can be described in multiple overlapping discovery rectangles but only once in the global independent-station ledger.
Geographic proximity and simultaneous measurements are NOT hydrological connectivity or a trained regional FCM.
Gage height without a documented common geodetic datum is not physically comparable across stations.
Region readiness depends on accumulated day-to-day aligned transitions; longest unbroken run is a separate reported metric.
At least three independent stations, source QA, reviewed connectivity and spatial holdouts are still required for regional FCM research.
